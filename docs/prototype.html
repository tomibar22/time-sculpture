How to use it
Select
Tap a group. Shift-click selects a range, Ctrl or ⌘-click adds one. On a phone, press and hold a group, or turn on “Select several”, then tap more.
Move
With Pulse or Beat, a dragged group (or a run of selected groups) slides along the grid in those steps. Whatever it runs into gives way: open space and silences shrink, sounding groups are shortened, and the time it leaves behind becomes silence. With Place, dragging reorders groups instead and the others make room. The arrow keys follow the same choice.
Reshape
Drag the line between two groups. Drag a green edge into the open space to stretch. To merge, select neighbouring groups and press Merge.
Split
Double-tap a group.
Subdivision
Pick 1:1 to 8:1, then tap or drag along the numbered lane above a row. Groups keep their moments in time and snap to the nearest new pulse; you can undo.
